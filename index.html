Vision Train · Research Convergence Series

Envisioning sustainable structures for Artists

What does it mean to be an artist now? in the future? What support do artists need? Over a series of research convergence sessions, artists will create together while learning from across-disciplinary guest who is both an artist and an expert in an emergent field. The resulting conversation will be the ground for envisioning a world where creation and animacy is protected and celebrated.
